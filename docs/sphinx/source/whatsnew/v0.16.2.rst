Bug fixes
~~~~~~~~~
* :py:func:`~pvlib.pvsystem.singlediode` with ``method='lambertw'`` no longer
  emits ``RuntimeWarning: invalid value encountered in divide`` when the open
  circuit voltage is zero, e.g. with zero photocurrent. (:issue:`2671`)

~~~~~~~~~~~~~
* Clarified in :py:func:`pvlib.tracking.singleaxis` that the tracker
  rotation minimizes the angle of incidence (AOI) to maximize the use of
  direct normal irradiance (DNI). (:issue:`2314`, :pull:`2869`)
